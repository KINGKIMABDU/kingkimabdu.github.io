About

More than a student.

I am Abdullah Alhariri. I am originally from Syria, now living in Germany, and online I usually go by KINGKIMABDU or 킹키마브두. I am the kind of person who gets curious fast when something has wires, code, science, or a clever idea behind it.

I like learning by taking things apart in my head first, then building something small to see if I really understood it. MINT subjects fit that perfectly: math, computer science, natural sciences, and technology all feel connected to how I think.

Tech is a big part of my life, but it is not the only part. I care a lot about health, fitness, bodybuilding, and discipline. I also enjoy FPV drones, gaming, and competitive play. In Valorant I main Neon because fast and energetic fits me well.

Green is my favorite color, K-pop is my favorite music genre, and coffee or matcha can fix a lot of long study days. I am still figuring things out, but I know I want to keep learning, stay healthy, build useful things, and become a doctor one day.
